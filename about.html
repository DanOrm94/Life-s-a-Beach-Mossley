About Life's A Beach

Life's A Beach is a much-loved Mossley salon on Manchester Road, offering a full mix of tanning, hair, nails, beauty and holistic spa treatments under one roof. The salon is known locally for its relaxed atmosphere, friendly team and great value saver offers.

With sunbeds on the ground floor and a spa space upstairs, it's designed as a one-stop destination where you can book everything from a quick gel manicure or brow tidy through to a full pamper package with massage and facials.
