Salon services

Below is an overview of the main treatment categories offered at Life's A Beach. For full menus, live prices and appointment availability, please use the Fresha booking link.

Facials & skincare

Classic facials

Deep-cleansing and hydrating facials tailored to your skin type, ideal for regular maintenance and relaxation.

Dermaplaning

Specialist exfoliating treatment that removes dead skin cells and peach fuzz to reveal a smoother, brighter complexion.

Targeted skin treatments

Options for congestion, dryness and radiance, using quality products chosen to suit individual concerns.

Nails & beauty

Manicures & pedicures

From classic polish to gel finishes, including callus softener and removal for smoother feet.

Gel polish & nail art

Gel colour with options for accents and simple nail art, perfect for everyday wear or occasions.

Brows, lashes & make-up

Brow shaping, tinting and lash treatments alongside make-up appointments to complete your look.

Massage & holistic spa

Aromatherapy massage

Relaxing massage using essential oils, ideal for stress relief and general wellbeing.

Deep muscle massage

Firmer pressure to work on areas of tension, perfect for those who prefer a stronger massage.

Pamper packages

Combined treatments such as massage and facial or full body rituals, making the most of the spa space upstairs.

Waxing & hair removal

Body waxing

Legs, arms, underarms and other areas with options for both strip and hot wax depending on your preference.

Intimate waxing

Discreet, professional intimate waxing carried out by experienced therapists in private treatment rooms.

Brows & facial hair removal

Brows, lip and chin waxing or other methods to tidy and shape facial hair.

Sunbeds & tanning

Stand-up and lay-down units

Modern sunbeds maintained to a high standard, offering both stand-up and lay-down options.

Saver minutes & deals

Regular deals such as "100 minutes for £30" and Saturday saver offers, advertised via social media.

Advice & guidance

Staff available to discuss suitable session lengths and tanning routines.

Hairdressing

Cut & blow-dry

Wash, cut and blow-dry appointments to keep styles fresh, with options to add keratin treatments.

Colouring & highlights

Full head colour and full head highlights, with toners available to refine the finished shade.

Styling for occasions

Blow-dries and styling for events, nights out and special occasions.
